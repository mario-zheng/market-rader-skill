各板块涨跌幅原因剖析


本周大部分覆盖板块延续上周趋势继续回调，部分新消费板块由于
市场前期对业绩预期过高且估值较高进入震荡回调阶段，如 黄金珠
宝板块 本周继续回调，平均下挫 4.8% 。 博彩板块 前期由于 6 月 GGR
超预期带动股价和业绩预期上调，至 7 月中澳门 GGR 同比增速仍为
高双位数增长，但环比 6 月增速有所下滑，日度 GGR 环比走弱，股
价也迎来调整，本周下跌 2.9% 。市场对暑假旅行旺季数据仍持乐
观态度，澳门密集安排的演唱会和演出是带动人流和 GGR 向上的
催化剂。 美国酒店 本周股价下挫 2.9% ，主要因为市场对后半年高
基数下业绩信心走弱，而非下调最近一个季度酒店业绩。美国 4-5
月因为经济波动和关税带来的不确定性酒店 Revpar 降速， 6 月
Revpar 数据也没有明显恢复，临近年中开始担忧下半年行业表现。
宠物板块 股价本周下挫 2.4% ，在乖宝调整二季度业绩预期后，市
场对宠物板块的业绩和估值仍有担忧。零食板块本周股价下挫
2.3% ，其中万辰股价下跌 5.5% ，主要因为市场担忧二季度业绩，
有友股价下跌 5.3% ，市场担心山姆供应链舆论事件影响有友后续
对山姆产品销售。 国内化妆品 板块公司股价本周普跌，其中上美股
份下跌 9.2% ，优于前期涨幅较多且估值较高，受到新消费概念股
波动影响。巨子生物本周股价上行 6.6% ，一方面因为 7 月抖音平台
自播间上线后日度销售数据表现超预期，另一方面因为近期风格切
换，从高估值高预期的新消费概念股切入低估值低预期的消费标
的。 奢侈品、信用卡、海外运动服饰和生活用品 板块本周股价波动
较小。

可选消费子板块估值分析 **:** 大多板块估值仍低于过去 **5** 年平均


海外运动服饰板块 2025 年预期 PE 为 34.5 倍，相当于过去 5 年平均预测 PE 的 55% ；
EV/EBITDA 为 24.1 。国内运动服饰板块 2025 年预期 PE 为 12.9 倍，相当于过去 5 年平
均预测 PE 的 74% ； EV/EBITDA 为 8.0 。黄金珠宝板块 2025 年预期 PE 为 27.2 倍，相当
于过去 5 年平均预测 PE 的 48% ； EV/EBITDA 为 12.4 。奢侈品板块 2025 年预期 PE 为
23.1 倍，相当于过去 5 年平均预测 PE 的 46% ； EV/EBITDA 为 12.5 。博彩板块 2025 年
预期 PE 为 17.0 倍，相当于过去 5 年平均预测 PE 的 26% ； EV/EBITDA 为 12.0 。海外化
妆品板块 2025 年预期 PE 为 40.4 倍，相当于过去 5 年平均预测 PE 的 63% ； EV/EBITDA
为 16.4 。国内化妆品板块 2025 年预期 PE 为 30.7 倍，相当于过去 5 年平均预测 PE 的
57% ； EV/EBITDA 为 24.5 。宠物板块 2025 年预期 PE 为 41.6 倍，相当于过去 5 年平均
预测 PE 的 45% ； EV/EBITDA 为 27.4 。零食板块 2025 年预期 PE 为 23.2 倍，相当于过去
5 年平均预测 PE 的 38% ； EV/EBITDA 为 15.6 。生活用品板块 2025 年预期 PE 为 23.1 倍，
相当于过去 5 年平均预测 PE 的 71% ； EV/EBITDA 为 17.7 。美国酒店板块 2025 年预期
PE 为 30.7 倍，相当于过去 5 年平均预测 PE 的 19% ； EV/EBITDA 为 21.1 。信用卡板块
2025 年预期 PE 为 30.8 倍，相当于过去 5 年平均预测 PE 的 61% ； EV/EBITDA 为 19.5 。
其中，各板块的 2025 年预期 PE 均低于历史五年平均 PE ，美国酒店 < 博彩 < 零食 < 宠物 <
奢侈品 < 黄金珠宝 < 海外运动服饰 < 国内化妆品 < 信用卡 < 海外化妆品 < 生活用品 < 国内运
动服饰；仅生活用品板块的 2025 年 EV/EBITDA 高于历史五年平均 EV/EBITDA 。

各板块涨跌幅原因剖析


本周大部分覆盖板块延续上周趋势继续回调，部分新消费板块由于市场前期对业绩预
期过高且估值较高进入震荡回调阶段，如 黄金珠宝板块 本周继续回调，平均下挫 4.8% 。
博彩板块 前期由于 6 月 GGR 超预期带动股价和业绩预期上调，至 7 月中澳门 GGR 同
比增速仍为高双位数增长，但环比 6 月增速有所下滑，日度 GGR 环比走弱，股价也迎
来调整，本周下跌 2.9% 。市场对暑假旅行旺季数据仍持乐观态度，澳门密集安排的演
唱会和演出是带动人流和 GGR 向上的催化剂。 美国酒店 本周股价下挫 2.9% ，主要因
为市场对后半年高基数下业绩信心走弱，而非下调最近一个季度酒店业绩。美国 4-5
月因为经济波动和关税带来的不确定性酒店 Revpar 降速， 6 月 Revpar 数据也没有明显
恢复，临近年中开始担忧下半年行业表现。 宠物板块 股价本周下挫 2.4% ，在乖宝调整
二季度业绩预期后，市场对宠物板块的业绩和估值仍有担忧。零食板块本周股价下挫
2.3% ，其中万辰股价下跌 5.5% ，主要因为市场担忧二季度业绩，有友股价下跌 5.3% ，
市场担心山姆供应链舆论事件影响有友后续对山姆产品销售。 国内化妆品 板块公司股
价本周普跌，其中上美股份下跌 9.2% ，优于前期涨幅较多且估值较高，受到新消费概
念股波动影响。巨子生物本周股价上行 6.6% ，一方面因为 7 月抖音平台自播间上线后
日度销售数据表现超预期，另一方面因为近期风格切换，从高估值高预期的新消费概
念股切入低估值低预期的消费标的。 奢侈品、信用卡、海外运动服饰和生活用品 板块
本周股价波动较小。本周 国内运动服饰 板块表现较好，上涨 1.4% ，运动品牌均为正向
表现，其中安踏上涨 5.5% ，因为二季度各品牌流水预期实际表现好于市场较低预期。
海外化妆品 板块本周表现最优，股价上涨 2.5% ，其中 E.L.F. Beauty 表现优异，上涨
11% ，主要因为有投行上调目标价，显著高于此前市场预期，推动股价走强。尤是
E.L.F. Beauty 计划于 8 月 1 日实施的关税应对定价策略已获得消费者和零售商积极反
馈。

**Style Shifts to Low-Valuation, Low-Expectation Consumption Stocks.**
The weekly performance of each sector is as follows: Overseas Cosmetics > Domestic Sportswear > Luxury Goods > Credit Card >

negative growth.

**Reasons for Sector Performance**
This week, most of the covered sectors continued the trend from last week and experienced a pullback. Some new-consumption
sectors, which had previously seen high performance expectations and valuations, entered a phase of volatility and correction. For
example, the Gold Jewelry sector continued to decline, with an average drop of 4.8%. The Gambling sector, which had seen its
stock prices and performance expectations rise due to the June GGR outperformance, faced a correction this week. Although
Macau's GGR maintained a high double-digit year-on-year growth in mid-July, the month-on-month growth rate declined from
June, and the daily GGR also weakened, leading to a 2.9% drop in stock prices. The market remains optimistic about the summer
travel peak season data, with Macau's densely scheduled concerts and performances acting as catalysts to drive foot traffic and
GGR upward.
The U.S. Hotel sector saw a 2.9% drop in stock prices this week, primarily due to weakening confidence in performance for the
second half of the year under a high base effect, rather than a downgrade of the most recent quarterly hotel performance. The
uncertainty caused by economic fluctuations and tariffs in April and May led to a slowdown in hotel RevPAR, and the June RevPAR
data showed no significant recovery. As the year's midpoint approaches, concerns about the industry's performance in the second
half are growing.
The Pet sector's stock prices fell by 2.4% this week. After the adjustment of second-quarter performance expectations by Gambol
Pet Group, the market still has concerns about the sector's performance and valuation. The Snacks sector declined by 2.3% this
week. Among them, Wanchen saw its stock price drop by 5.5%, mainly due to market concerns about second-quarter performance.
Youyou Food fell by 5.3% as the market worried that the supply chain controversy involving Sam's Club would affect its subsequent
product sales to Sam's Club.
The Domestic Cosmetics sector saw a general decline in stock prices this week. Among them, Shangmei fell by 9.2%, partly due to
its previous significant gains and high valuation, which were affected by the volatility of new-consumption concept stocks. Giant
Biogene saw its stock price rise by 6.6% this week. On one hand, the daily sales data from its self-operated live-streaming room on
the Douyin platform in July exceeded expectations. On the other hand, the recent market style shift from high-valuation, highexpectation new-consumption concept stocks to low-valuation, low-expectation consumer stocks also contributed to its
performance.
The Luxury Goods, Credit Card, Overseas Sportswear, and Daily Necessities sectors saw relatively small stock price fluctuations this
week. The Domestic Sportswear sector performed well this week, rising by 1.4%. All sportswear brands showed positive
performance, with Anta rising by 5.5%, as the actual performance of various brand sales in the second quarter exceeded the
market's relatively low expectations. The Overseas Cosmetics sector had the best performance this week, with a 2.5% increase in
stock prices. Among them, E.L.F. Beauty performed exceptionally well, rising by 11%. This was mainly due to an investment bank
raising its target price significantly above previous market expectations, driving the stock price upward. E.L.F. Beauty's tariff
response pricing strategy, which is scheduled to be implemented on August 1st, has already received positive feedback from
consumers and retailers.
