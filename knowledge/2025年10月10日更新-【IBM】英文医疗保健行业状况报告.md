### Executive summary



Healthcare organizations face unprecedented pressure to balance cost containment with expanding Patient/Member and
Provider expectations. As digital adoption accelerates—and agentic AI emerges as a pivotal enabler—organizations must
embrace an end-to-end transformation to create a holistic, patient-centered, sustainable system.


Even as medical and technological breakthroughs flourish, geopolitical complexities introduce new challenges through supply
chain vulnerabilities, regulatory fragmentation, and increasingly nationalistic healthcare policies. Sustainable healthcare
requires executives to strategically navigate cost optimization while delivering personalized care in a diverse, politically
influenced global landscape.


Based on research from IBM Institute for Business Value studies and the expertise of our industry leaders, we offer a high-level
view of the healthcare industry today with a focus on the impacts of major technologies. Select case studies demonstrate how
companies are successfully using advanced technologies to improve operations and the patient experiences.

in healthcare.

organizations gain flexibility to maintain continuous operations, scale resources, enhance security,
and deliver better patient care.

- Cloud infrastructure scales easily as the hospital grows.

- Leading cloud providers enable compliance with regulations such as HIPAA, GDPR, and so on,

protect patient data.
