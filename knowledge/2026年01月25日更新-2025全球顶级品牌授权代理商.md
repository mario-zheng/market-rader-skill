### **LICENSING** AGENTS 2025



**THE BUSINESS MODEL** of brand
licensing demands cohesive and
symbiotic partnerships. Brand licensing
agents strategically guide brands
into new markets through their vast
licensee networks, fostering authentic
partnerships to build engagement across
numerous channels. These relationships
keep global IPs fresh and expansive,
broadening connections between the
brand and consumers.

Finding the correct partners to bring an
IP to life through a consumer product is a
job all on its own, and that’s where brand
licensing agents shine. As the consumer
landscape continues to shift, brand
licensing agents facilitate opportunities so
the industry can continue to flourish.

By highlighting the successes of agents
in the industry, The Top Global Licensing
Agents report functions as an annual
snapshot of what has been and what is
yet to come in the industry through the
eyes of some of its most influential entities.
Additionally, it acts as a directory for
licensing professionals in need of agency
expertise, and it serves as an overall
outlook on the industry, with invaluable
qualitative and quantitative data.


**OUR METHODOLOGY**

License Global continues to use the
original methodology set out during the
conception of these reports. First, we invite
brand licensing agencies worldwide to
participate in the report by filling out an
open survey. This survey includes essential
quantitative information, including retail
sales of licensed consumer products, as

categories, regional growth, generational

licensor, a licensee, a service provider
or a fellow agent, this report will have
something of use to you.



well as insights into industry trends to
build a comprehensive forecast for the
coming years. With all data compiled,
the License Global team validates
this information by cross-checking
submissions and speaking with agencies
directly over any discrepancies where
appropriate. From there, data retrieved
and collated results in a ranking based
on submitted revenues. Sixty slots were

to grow, with new agents and established

**HOW TO ENSURE BRANDS**

**AI AND DATA-DRIVEN**
DECISION-MAKING

AI is transforming brand licensing,
making data-driven strategies essential
for success. Licensing agents must utilize
AI-powered insights to analyze consumer
behavior, predict market trends and
optimize deals. Automation streamlines
operations, from contract negotiations to
royalty management, ensuring efficiency
and cost-effectiveness. Integrating
AI into workflows enables agents to
make informed decisions, reduce risks
and uncover new opportunities. As
competition grows, agents who don’t
leverage AI will struggle to keep up with
rapidly evolving market demands.



**DIGITAL** AND
**E-COMMERCE** EXPANSION

With online retail dominating consumer
shopping habits, brand licensing agents
must prioritize digital and e-commerce
strategies. Establishing a strong online
presence through direct-to-consumer
(DTC) models, marketplaces, and social
commerce can open new revenue
streams. Omnichannel retail, which
seamlessly integrates online and offline
experiences, is also crucial for ensuring
that brands remain accessible across all
platforms. In fact, this should be the norm.
If you do not have a digital or e-comm
strategy, you are behind.



SUSTAINABILITY AND
**ETHICAL LICENSING**

Sustainability is increasingly vital
as a differentiator. Consumers and
stakeholders now expect brands to
prioritize ethical sourcing, eco-friendly
packaging and transparent supply chains.
Licensing agents must ensure alignment
with partners who embrace these values
by incorporating sustainability clauses
into agreements. By adopting sustainable
practices, brands can bolster their
reputation, foster consumer trust and
safeguard licensing strategies against
evolving environmental regulations. This
commitment is essential for long-term
success and market relevance.

GLOBAL **BRAND**
**COLLABORATIONS**

Strategic partnerships and co-branding
initiatives remain powerful tools for
expanding market reach and creating
unique consumer experiences. Licensing
agents should seek cross-industry
collaborations to enhance brand equity
and attract new audiences. From limitededition product drops to long-term
alliances, partnerships drive innovation,
exclusivity and consumer engagement.
Meanwhile, global expansion into
emerging markets unlocks growth
potential and new revenue streams. To
succeed, agents must understand local
cultures, regulatory environments and
consumer behaviors, while ensuring
compliance with international trade laws.



SUPPORT AND
**CUSTOMER**
**ENGAGEMENT**

Building strong customer relationships
is essential for long-term brand success.
Licensing agents must prioritize consumer
engagement strategies that drive brand
loyalty and advocacy. This includes
personalized marketing, interactive brand
experiences and direct engagement
through social media and digital channels.
Offering excellent post-purchase support
and community-driven initiatives
strengthens trust and encourages repeat
business. In a time when brand perception
is shaped by customer interactions,
prioritizing engagement is key to
maintaining a competitive edge and
fostering long-term growth.



ADAPTABILITY AND
**FLEXIBILITY**

The licensing industry is rapidly
evolving, demanding a flexible
approach to agreements. Traditional
long-term contracts may no longer
be ideal as market trends shift
quickly. Licensing agents must
create agile agreements that allow
brands to pivot when needed.
This includes adopting dynamic
pricing, performance-based royalties
and short-term partnerships
that enable experimentation. By
staying adaptable, brands can seize
emerging trends and meet evolving
consumer demands, ensuring they
remain competitive and responsive
to market changes.
