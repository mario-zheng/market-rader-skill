## CATEGORY CONVERGENCE


CATEGORY CONVERGENCE delves into how consumers
are not only looking to streamline their beauty routines, but
are also expecting more from multi-tasking products. That’s
seen in color getting infused into skin care and hair care;
skin care becoming clinical-level; and holidays combining
with cosmetic surgery.

## RAW BEAUTY


RAW BEAUTY explores a move toward a straightforward,
more sustainable beauty, in which concepts are explained
simply. Cue transparency around ingredients, while
packaging is intuitive and low-key, to counter a fatigue that’s
surrounding excess in beauty.
