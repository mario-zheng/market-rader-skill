#### **Hallucination Rates of AI Models**



When asked to identify and cite
news sources from an excerpt,
many of today’s AI models
confidently delivered wrong
answers. They rarely signaled
uncertainty or said “I don’t know.”


AI hallucinates because standard
training systems reward guessing
over showing uncertainty. Think
about it this way: if you guess on
a multiple choice test, you are
more likely to get it right than
if you give no answer.


This confidence illusion misleads
teams and magnifies risk. And
with AI agents—where every
action builds on the last—each
mistake compounds.
