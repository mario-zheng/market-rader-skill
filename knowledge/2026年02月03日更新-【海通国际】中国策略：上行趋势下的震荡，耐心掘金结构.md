2026 年初以来，上证综指先后突破 4000 点与 4100 点关口，并逼近 4200 点。市场开始呈现一定程度的过热现象： 1 ）商业
航天与 AI 应用板块的火爆氛围带动增量资金集中入场，周一成交额大幅放量至 3.6 万亿元并创历史新高， ETF 批量涨停（包
括多只规模超百亿的 ETF ）。 2 ）杠杆资金加速流入 —— 本周前四个交易日两融资金净流入达 912 亿，且已连续两周大幅净
流入，流入强度与 2025 年 8–9 月阶段性高位相当；两融余额突破 2.7 万亿，融资买入额占比升至 11% ，其一年分位数在周
一达到 94% （图 1 、 2 ）。 3 ）散户资金流入持续攀升（图 3 ）。


在此背景下， 1 月 15 日，中国证监会召开 2026 年系统工作会议，分析当前形势，研究部署 2026 年工作，会议强调，坚持
稳字当头、巩固市场稳中向好势头，及时做好逆周期调节、坚决防止市场大起大落。相应组合拳包括： 1 ）沪深北交易所
将融资保证金最低比例由 80% 上调至 100% 。 2 ） 1 月 15 日 A 股市场 8 只宽基 ETF 单日净流出资金达 668.09 亿元，创历史最
高纪录，其中华泰柏瑞沪深 300ETF 单日净流出 201.57 亿元，为成立以来最大流出规模。宽基 ETF 估算周四、周五合计流
出资金约 1700 亿；作为对比， 2025 年全年净流入约 500 亿，且 2025 年 4 月 “ 对等关税战 ” 阶段买入约 1500 亿（图 4 ）。
3 ）周末彭博社报道，中国正将高频交易者的服务器从交易所本地数据中心迁出。我们认为这将 “ 进一步维护交易公平性，
严肃查处过度炒作乃至操纵市场等违法违规行为 ” ，短期或将压制投机情绪，但不会改变中期行情的趋势。


市场展望 ：伴随监管短期 “ 降温 ” 意图的强化， A 股前期领涨板块如商业航天本周批量跌停，短期情绪面或需一定时间修
复，市场或将进入阶段性震荡。但是，中期而言，慢牛的大趋势是监管层和市场主导型资金的一致目标和共同利益，春季
行情并没有结束，而是洗洗更健康，后续在消化短期结构性过热风险后，行情将继续向上。


投资策略 ：在短期震荡阶段，建议保持耐心，但中期多头思维不改，把握住这个震荡调整的时间窗口，积极挖掘更具中长
期性价比的中国资产。 A 股和港股长期而言是相辅相成、共同促进中国资产在全球财富配置中的崛起；中短期的行情节奏
则是你追我赶、交替领跑，短期考虑到 A 股严监管及情绪 “ 降温 ” ，而港股科技权重股后续将受益于进入 AI 产品密集发布窗
口，所以， “ 北水 ” 或有望更积极掘金港股的优势资产，短期港股或有望强于 A 股。


立足春季行情，重点不在于指数的短期起伏，关键在于结构，建议投资者顺应 “ 抑制题材炒作，促进上市公司价值成长和
治理提升 ” 的监管意图，趁行情震荡而深挖半导体、创新药、港股科技龙头、光模块等结构性亮点，增加港股优势红利资
产的配置，等待 A 股航天军工等热门题材赛道调整之后的分化机会：


     - 半导体 ：台积电 2026 年资本开支指引达 520-560 亿美元，占其上一年经营现金流超过 100% （图 5 ），此前海力士规划投
入 19 万亿韩元建设先进封装工厂，强化 AI 需求的可见度。本周 A 股对投机资金的监管趋严，推动资金转向由机构主导的
半导体主线。国产半导体除与全球产业链共振外，后续还将受益于国产替代驱动的扩产增量：国产晶圆龙头自 2020 年起
在国产替代窗口下抬升资本开支并持续高位运行， 2025Q3 因设备到货带动资本开支再上台阶， 4Q25-1Q26 投产兑现、产能
持续爬坡（图 6 ）；同时，长鑫等大型科技 IPO 亦有望形成阶段性催化。


     - 创新药 ：医药板块近 4 个月南向资金合计流出 43 亿，与 2025 年 6-9 月大幅流入 740 亿形成明显对比（图 7 ），资金面已
经完成 “ 去拥挤 ” 。与此同时，创新药仍处于政策加码周期：医保局发布的新版国家医保药品目录纳入 50 种 1 类创新药，并
同步公布商业健康保险创新药品目录；药监局优化创新药审评审批，鼓励临床急需、境外已上市的原研药及仿制药在境内
申报。


     - 港股科技龙头 ：加速推进 AI 与业务生态融合，阿里以千问为底座在电商、出行等核心场景推进产品化落地， Agent 应用
有望持续扩围；腾讯依托微信生态，通过 Agent 平台降低开发门槛，推动 AI 小程序与智能体持续丰富，并在教育等垂直领
域实现规模化应用。此外，阿里云海外业务增长动能强劲；百度考虑将港股上市地位升级为主要上市，以获纳入港股通资
格。港股层面还聚集了 H 股半导体龙头，叠加近期部分国产 GPU 、大模型、硬科技等公司上市后表现稳健，后续优质科技
企业赴港节奏有望延续，为港股科技提供持续的增量标的。


     - 光模块 ：板块前期涨幅较大后转入高位横盘，后续仍需业绩继续验证以推动趋势延续；但短期成交占比已回落至前期峰
值的一半（图 8 ）。当前 A 股进入年报预告期，叠加场内情绪资金退潮，资金转向业绩确定性方向，或有望回流至相对滞
涨的光模块板块。


风险提示：地缘冲突与贸易摩擦风险；美联储降息不及预期风险；中国经济复苏节奏和化债风险。

**Summary**


Since the beginning of2026, the Shanghai Composite has broken above the4,000 and4,100 thresholds in succession and is now
approaching4,200. The market has started to show signs of overheating to some extent: (1) The exuberant sentiment in
commercial space and AI application themes drew a surge of incremental inflows. On Monday, turnover jumped to RMB3.6trn,
a record high, and a broad batch of ETFs hit limit-up moves (including multiple ETFs with AUM aboveRMB10bn). (2) Leveraged
capital accelerated into the market—net margin financing inflows totaledRMB91.2bn over the first four trading days of the
week, marking a second consecutive week of heavy inflows, comparable to the cyclical highs in Aug–Sep2025. Outstanding
margin balances rose aboveRMB2.7trn; margin purchase turnover increased to11% of total turnover, with its one-year
percentile reaching94% on Monday (Figures1&2). (3) Retail inflows continued to climb (Figure3).


Against this backdrop, on January15 the China Securities Regulatory Commission (CSRC) convened its2026 system-wide work
conference to assess current conditions and set priorities for2026. The meeting emphasized a “stability-first” approach,
consolidating the momentum of a steadily improving market, conducting timely countercyclical adjustments, and resolutely
preventing boom-bust swings. The corresponding policy package includes: (1) The Shanghai, Shenzhen, and Beijing stock
exchanges raised the minimum margin financing collateral ratio from80% to100%. (2) On January15, eight broad-based A-share
ETFs recorded net outflows of RMB66.809bn in a single day, the highest on record. Among them, the Huatai-PineBridge
CSI300ETF saw a one-day net outflow of RMB20.157bn, the largest since its inception. We estimate that broad-based ETFs saw
combined outflows of roughlyRMB170bn on Thursday and Friday; by comparison, net inflows for full-year2025 were
aboutRMB50bn, while purchases during the April2025 “reciprocal tariff war” episode totaled aroundRMB150bn (Figure4). (3)
Bloomberg reported over the weekend that China is relocating high-frequency traders’ servers out of exchange on-site data
centers. In our view, this will “further safeguard trading fairness and rigorously investigate illegal activities such as excessive
speculation and even market manipulation.” It may dampen speculative sentiment in the near term, but it should not alter the
medium-term trend.


**Market Outlook** : As the regulator’s near-term “cooling” intent intensifies, previously leading themes such as commercial space
saw waves of limit-down moves this week. Sentiment may require time to repair, and the market could enter a phase of
consolidation. Medium term, however, a slow-bull trend is a shared objective—and a common interest—of both regulators and
the market’s dominant long-term capital. The spring rally has not ended; rather, a “washout” can make the uptrend healthier.
After short-term structural overheating risks are digested, the market is likely to resume its upward trajectory.


**Investment Strategy** : During this near-term consolidation, we recommend staying patient while keeping our medium-term
bullish stance intact. Use this consolidation window to proactively identify Chinese assets with stronger medium-to-long-term
risk-reward. Over the long run, A-shares and Hong Kong equities are complementary and together can support the rising weight
of China assets in global allocation. In the near-to-medium term, however, their performance leadership typically rotates. Given
tighter A-share regulation and near-term sentiment cooling, while Hong Kong’s tech heavyweights are set to benefit from an
upcoming window of dense AI product launches, southbound flows may increasingly look to Hong Kong for high-quality
opportunities. As such, Hong Kong equities may outperform A-shares in the near term.


Within the spring rally framework, the key is not short-term index fluctuations but market structure. We suggest aligning with
the regulatory intent to “curb theme-based speculation and promote value growth and governance improvement among listed
companies.” Use market volatility to dig into structural opportunities in semiconductors, innovative drugs, Hong Kong tech
bellwethers, and optical modules, while increasing exposure to Hong Kong’s advantaged high-dividend assets. Meanwhile, wait
for differentiation opportunities after popular A-share themes such as aerospace/defense complete their correction:


   - **Semiconductors** : TSMC’s2026 capex guidance stands at US$52–56bn, exceeding100% of its prior-year operating cash flow
(Figure5). Earlier, SK hynix announced plans to investKRW19trn to build an advanced packaging plant, strengthening visibility
into AI demand. This week, tighter A-share scrutiny of speculative capital helped rotate flows toward the institution-led
semiconductor theme. Beyond resonating with the global cycle, China’s domestic semiconductor chain should further benefit
from capacity expansion driven by import substitution: leading domestic foundries have raised capex since2020 under an
import-substitution window and have maintained elevated spending; capex stepped up again in2025Q3 as equipment deliveries
arrived, with commissioning in4Q25–1Q26 and continued ramp-up in capacity (Figure6). In addition, large tech IPOs such as
CXMT could provide incremental catalysts.

   - **Innovative drugs** : Over the past four months, southbound flows into the healthcare sector turned to a net outflow
ofRMB4.3bn, in sharp contrast to net inflows ofRMB74.0bn during Jun–Sep2025 (Figure7), indicating that positioning has
already de-crowded. Meanwhile, innovative drugs remain in a policy tailwind cycle: the National Healthcare Security
Administration (NHSA) released an updated National Reimbursement Drug List including50 Category1 innovative drugs, and
simultaneously introduced a commercial health insurance innovative drug catalog. The National Medical Products
Administration (NMPA) is also optimizing review and approval processes for innovative drugs, encouraging domestic filings for
clinically urgent originator drugs and generics that have already been marketed overseas.


  - **Hong Kong tech leaders** : Leading companies are accelerating AI integration across their ecosystems. Alibaba is using Qwen as
the foundation to push productization in core scenarios such as e-commerce and local services/travel, with Agent applications
likely to expand further. Tencent, leveraging the WeChat ecosystem, is lowering development barriers through its Agent
platform, continuously enriching AI mini programs and agents, and achieving scaled adoption in verticals such as education. In
addition, Alibaba Cloud’s overseas business is showing strong growth momentum; Baidu is considering upgrading its Hong Kong
listing status to a primary listing in order to qualify for inclusion in Stock Connect. Hong Kong also hosts H-share semiconductor
leaders. Together with the steady post-listing performance of several recently listed domestic GPU, foundation model, and
“hard-tech” companies, the pipeline of high-quality tech listings in Hong Kong is likely to continue, providing a sustained flow
of incremental investable names for the Hong Kong tech complex.


   - **CPO** : After strong early gains, the sector has shifted into a high-level consolidation, and further earnings validation is needed
to extend the trend. That said, its turnover share has already fallen to about half of its prior peak (Figure8). As A-shares enter
the annual results pre-announcement window and sentiment-driven capital fades, flows are rotating toward earnings
certainty—potentially benefiting relatively lagging optical module names.


**Risks:** geopolitical conflicts and trade-friction risks; the risk that Fed easing falls short of expectations; risks related to the pace
of China’s economic recovery and the debt-resolution (deleveraging) process.
