###### **About this study**

_**The State of the Generative AI Market: Widespread Transformation Continues**_ study,
conducted by TechTarget’s Enterprise Strategy Group analysts in July 2024, surveyed 832
global IT and business professionals involved in AI decision-making to provide a
comprehensive view of the evolving market.


**The study covers:**

1. The current state of GenAI, including adoption, budget and implementation strategies.

2. Current and planned GenAI operational approaches, best practices and use cases.

3. Key challenge areas with GenAI.

4. Planned areas of investment and key focus areas for GenAI technologies.


For deeper insight on buying teams and change dynamics, or the IT buyer journey and content
consumption preferences, **[contact us](https://www.techtarget.com/talk-to-an-expert/?utm_source=ebook&utm_campaign=the-state-of-the-generative-ai-market)** to request our other studies:

###### **Key findings**

**68% of orgs plan to increase their**
**GenAI budgets over the next 12**
**months.** GenAI use cases are
among the top researched topics on
the TechTarget network.


**Key areas of investment to support**
**GenAI initiatives include:**

  - Training (45%),

  - Information management (41%)

  - Data privacy/compliance (40%)

  - Cybersecurity (37%)



**Orgs are applying GenAI in an average**
**of 3.5 areas of the business.** Most
commonly applied in software
development (41%), research (39%) and
IT operations (35%).


**Data Privacy and Security are top**
**concerns (73%).** Organizations are
focused on cybersecurity, investing in
solutions and implementing guardrails.
Additionally, 78% say their orgs are
prioritizing responsible AI.



**IT leadership and data scientists are**
**most involved in GenAI buying teams,**
with significant contributions from IT
management and cybersecurity.


**54% of organizations are more likely to**
**consider vendors that incorporate GenAI**
**features and functionality,** reflecting the
value placed on AI-enhanced products
and services.
