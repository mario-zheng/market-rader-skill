## 5. International internal audit activities updates

**The** **Institute of Internal Auditors (IIA)**


**Open for public comment: Organizational behavior topical requirement**


     - In July, the IIA released _the draft Organizational Behavior Topical Requirement_ and opened for public comment


through 22 August. The Topical Requirement aims to reframe culture risk as the risk of behavior misaligned


with strategic objectives, thereby providing internal auditors with a clear framework and minimum mandatory


criteria for assessing organizational behavior risks, mainly including organizational behavior governance, risk


management and control.


**2026 Risk in Focus report**


IIA released 2026 Risk in Focus report based on survey results, regional roundtables and interviews with local


experts to reveal key insights about regional risks along with perspective on risks worldwide.


     - In September, released IIA news indicated that the new report reveals geopolitical uncertainty and digital


disruption risks on the rise, marking largest increase in risk level this year. To manage abovementioned risks,


audit functions are adapting by expanding advisory roles, joining cross-functional teams and integrating into


strategic planning. Meanwhile, organizations has been developing AI governance teams. Internal auditors has


also been using AI to boost efficiency and data insights.


However, the misalignment exists between risk levels and audit priorities, such as geopolitical uncertainty,


digital disruption and human capital, highlighting the need for greater audit focus on these areas. Current top


audit priorities are cybersecurity, governance and corporate reporting, business reliance, regulatory change


and financial liquidity.


The report underscores the need for internal audit to evolve proactively, by moving beyond compliance to


strategic risk leadership in an increasingly volatile and digital world.


     - The _2026 Risk in Focus – Hot topics for internal auditors (Asia Pacific)_ provides highlights that the region


shares a clear concern over growing geopolitical uncertainty. Simultaneously, organizations face unrelenting


threats from cybersecurity and new technology. In addition, for East Asian, Human capital is the top risk in

the region’s top five, and business resilience and cybersecurity are both high-risk and high-priority areas.


Audit functions are adapting by deeply understanding business strategy, testing supply chain reliability,


monitoring technology adoption, ensuring compliance, supporting new strategies and tracking industry trends,


to strengthen the capability. Internal audit strategies emphasize proactive engagement, forward-looking


insights, and consultative support, including advising governance bodies on uncertainty, assisting


organizations to thrive in a rapidly changing environment.
