**Water Resource Asset Account — Water availability across basins**
The Water Resource Asset Account documents the physical stock and flows of water at the
basin and provincial scale, using three complementary approaches: reported basin statistics
for three key basins; monthly provincial accounts using rainfall and evapotranspiration
modelling; and national basin-level accounts using ERA5-Land global reanalysis data covering
2000–2025.


The three basin accounts demonstrate both the scale of water availability in Lao PDR and the
profound differences across basins. The Numnguem is primarily a rainfall-driven system with
modest irrigation demand; the Sekong and Nam Ou receive large upstream tributary
contributions and are dominated by hydropower abstraction. These differences mean that
